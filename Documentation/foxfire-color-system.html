Foundation seven plus these eight is everything a flyer, newsletter, or Canva template needs. The full Supporting Palette above remains the system of record for the website and illustration work.

Usage

Supplement only. A layout should read as the Foundation seven first. If a composition leans supporting, rebalance toward the Foundation rather than adding more steps.

Approach, don't arrive. Steps stop short of their anchors: the last step of every family keeps clear visual distance from its Foundation color and stays visible against Starlight, so the Foundation remains the signature expression and every swatch stays a usable tool. The Illumination gradients are the one place a family touches its destination... gradients arrive, swatches never do.

Text. Body text is always Nightfall on light grounds and Starlight on dark grounds. Twilight 1 and Twilight 2 may set secondary text and captions. Display and eyebrow type on Starlight may use Nocturnal 1–3, Moonrise 1–2, or Golden Hour 1–2. No other supporting color sets type. Afterglow itself never sets type below display size on Starlight (3.87:1).

Tone-on-tone. Keep a graphic inside one family; its anchors are part of the family. Families are the containers that make tone-on-tone safe for non-designers.

De-emphasis. Rules, dividers, borders, form fields, and disabled states come from Twilight 3–5, never from the chromatic families.

States. On the web, hover and active shift one step darker within the same family.

Illumination gradients. Hero and storytelling moments only, never UI chrome. Rebuild each gradient from its recipe so the middle keeps its chroma. A gradient may only sit on a ground it passes; the bands above show every sanctioned pairing, and Nocturnal is Starlight-only. Type over a gradient sits on its dark third, in Starlight. The flat UI around a gradient draws from that gradient's own family.

Scarcity. Firefly and Foxfire 5 are the lit end of the system... the ramp holds cold, then ignites. They read as emission because they are rare. The moment they become wallpaper, the metaphor is gone.

No pigment enters the system from outside the Foundation seven. Every supporting color is a blend of two Foundation colors, or a Foundation color walked toward ink or Starlight. That single rule is what was worth taking from Firefox.
