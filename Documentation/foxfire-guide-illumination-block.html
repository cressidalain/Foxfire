Color Landscape

Illumination — the gradients

The Illumination gradients are the palette in motion. Each one is a Foundation journey rendered continuously: night deepening toward water, moonlight rising out of the dark, the glow arriving at the end of the blue, the ember warming into afterglow. Our solid colors approach these destinations; only the gradients arrive.

That is why a gradient is an event, not a background. Use one where the story turns — a hero area, a cover, a section opening, a banner that needs the night to move — and let the rest of the page hold still around it. One gradient per view. When arrival is everywhere, it stops meaning anything.

The rules

Where they live

Hero areas, covers, section dividers, and storytelling graphics — the brand's arrival moments. Never in UI, never on buttons, never as letterfills, never behind body text, and never in everyday member materials; gradient work is steward work.

One per view

A single gradient per page, spread, or screen. The scarcity that makes Firefly mean something governs the gradients too.

Grounds are sanctioned, not chosen

A gradient sits only on a ground it passes — the badges above are the complete list. Nocturnal never sits on Nightfall.

The company they keep

Flat UI and accents around a gradient draw from that gradient's own family, so the moment stays one journey rather than a collage.

Exactly these recipes

The four builds above are the only gradients in the system. No homemade blends, no re-stopped variations, no new directions of travel.

In print

Gradients band on modest presses. Physical reproductions get proofed by the brand steward before they ship.
