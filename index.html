What Foxfire is

Foxfire is the old Appalachian name for the glow of certain fungi on fallen wood — faint, patient, and visible only to those who walk at night.

Every course at Foxfire is taught by a member family and shaped by the students in the room. We meet weekly, we teach each other, and we let the kids set the pace.
